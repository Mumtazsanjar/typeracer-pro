About TypeRacer Pro

A free, beautiful, and feature-rich typing speed test built for everyone.

🎯 What is TypeRacer Pro?

TypeRacer Pro is a free, browser-based typing speed test designed to help you measure and improve your typing speed. Whether you're a student, developer, writer, or anyone who uses a keyboard — faster and more accurate typing saves you time every day.

With real-time feedback, letter-by-letter highlighting, and persistent score history, TypeRacer Pro gives you everything you need in a clean, distraction-free interface.

⚙️ How It Works

1
Choose your time — Pick 30, 60, or 120 seconds.
2
Start typing — The timer begins on your first keystroke.
3
Get results — See your WPM, accuracy, correct words, and rating.
4
Improve — Practice daily and track your progress in the history section.

📈 Understanding Your Results

⚡ Expert 100+ WPM — Top 1% of typists worldwide
🚀 Professional 70–99 WPM — Faster than most office workers
🔥 Fast 50–69 WPM — Well above average
✅ Good 35–49 WPM — Slightly above average
📈 Average 20–34 WPM — Room to improve
🌱 Beginner 0–19 WPM — Just getting started

💡 Tips to Type Faster

  • Use all 10 fingers (touch typing)
  • Look at the screen, not the keyboard
  • Prioritize accuracy over speed first
  • Practice for 10–15 minutes daily
  • Keep your wrists relaxed and raised
  • Use a mechanical keyboard if possible

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Simple, honest, and transparent.

📋 Overview

TypeRacer Pro is a client-side only web application. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

✅ We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server.

💾 Data We Store Locally

TypeRacer Pro stores your typing test history in your browser's localStorage. This data:

  • Stays on your device only — never sent to any server
  • Includes: WPM score, accuracy, correct/incorrect words, test duration, and timestamp
  • Is limited to your last 10 test results
  • Can be deleted at any time using the "Clear All" button in the test page
  • Is automatically cleared when you clear your browser data

🍪 Cookies

TypeRacer Pro does not use cookies of any kind — no tracking cookies, no analytics cookies, no session cookies.

The only browser storage used is localStorage for your test history and theme preference, both of which are stored locally.

📡 Third-Party Services

TypeRacer Pro uses the following third-party services for fonts only:

  • Google Fonts — Loads the Inter font. Google may log font requests per their own privacy policy.

No analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services are used.

👶 Children's Privacy

TypeRacer Pro is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else, as no personal data is collected at all.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date. Since we collect no data, changes are unlikely to affect users in any significant way.

📬 Contact

Have questions about this privacy policy? Since TypeRacer Pro is an open-source project with no backend, feel free to inspect the source code directly. All logic runs in your browser.
